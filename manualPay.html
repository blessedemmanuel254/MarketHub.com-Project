How to Pay

Complete the following steps on your phone.

  1. 1
    Open M-PESA

    Open the M-PESA menu on your mobile phone.

  2. 2
    Select Lipa na M-PESA

    Choose the option for making a business payment.

  3. 3
    Select Buy Goods and Services

    Choose Buy Goods and Services from the menu.

  4. 4
    Enter the Till Number

    Enter 1774150 as the Till Number.

  5. 5
    Confirm the Business Name

    Check that the displayed business name is MAKETHUB TALES before proceeding.

  6. 6
    Enter the Amount

    Enter the exact amount you intend to pay.

  7. 7
    Confirm Your Payment

    Enter your M-PESA PIN and confirm the transaction.

  8. 8
    Keep the Confirmation Message

    Save the M-PESA confirmation SMS and transaction code as proof of payment.

Important Payment Reminder Always verify the Till Number and business name before confirming. Never share your M-PESA PIN with anyone.
